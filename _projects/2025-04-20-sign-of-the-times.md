Every year, Ipsos B&A sets out to understand the mood of the nation -- to capture what life in Ireland really feels like. Through conversations, data, and insights, we build a picture of the trends shaping how people live, work, and connect. This year’s research explored how people experience life in Ireland today -- with a focus on technology, health and wellbeing, and the changing world of work.

Process: This project take place over the course of 4 months, moving through 3 accumulative phases:

<ol>
  <li>Qualitative: Expert in depth interview across sectors such as technology, hospitality, education and healthcare.</li>
  <li>Qualitative: Building on the insights from the expert phase we conducted consumer focus groups.</li>
  <li>Quantitative: Lastly, we conducted a survey. We used the insights from both qualitative phases to inform the survey design and the analysis.</li>
</ol>

The output is that the findings were published in the Irish Times, to read the article click [here](https://www.irishtimes.com/life-style/2025/04/20/ireland-now-anxious-jumpy-and-no-country-for-young-men-or-women/).
